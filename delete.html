Delete your Boda Rush data

Boda Rush: Dar (Android game by RingleSoft, package com.ringlesoft.bodarush)

How to request deletion

  1. Open the game, go to Scores, and note your rider name as it appears there.
  2. Send an email to eighty9nine@gmail.com with the subject "Boda Rush data deletion" and your rider name (and, if you can, your best score so we can find the right entry).
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your entries on the worldwide leaderboard: rider name, its random tag, scores and ride distances.

What is kept

  • Nothing else is held by us. There are no accounts, emails or passwords.
  • Everything else (coins, keys, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings, to delete it immediately.
  • Advertising data is held by Google AdMob, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads); see Google's Privacy Policy.
