Privacy Policy

Boda Rush: Dar (Android game, package com.ringlesoft.bodarush), by RingleSoft

Effective: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Boda Rush: Dar ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game has no sign-up and no accounts. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information you give us

Rider name. On first launch the game asks for a rider name (up to 12 letters). It can be a nickname; please do not use your full real name. It is shown on the in-game leaderboards.

2. Information stored on your phone

Your rider name, scores, coins, keys, upgrades, missions, settings and garage choices are saved only on your device. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Worldwide leaderboard (SilentWolf)

When you finish a ride, the game sends the following to our leaderboard service, SilentWolf, so it can show the worldwide top scores:

DataPurpose
Your rider name and a random 6-character tag created on your phone (not linked to your identity)To show your entry on the leaderboard and tell two riders with the same name apart
Your score and the distance of that rideTo rank the leaderboard

Your rider name and best score are visible to all players of the game. Like any web service, SilentWolf receives your device's IP address when the game connects; we do not use it to identify you. Scores are kept while the game is offered. To have your leaderboard entry removed, email us with your rider name (see Contact).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to continue after a crash or to earn a key). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (ads shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Depending on your consent choices and region, ads may be personalised. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

The game does not currently sell anything. If in-app purchases are added later, they will be processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with the service providers above (SilentWolf for the leaderboard, Google AdMob for ads), or if required by law.

7. Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we only send the small amount of data described above.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information (for example a real name on the leaderboard), contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use a nickname as your rider name. You can change it at any time on the Leaderboard screen.
  • Play offline: without an internet connection, no scores are sent and no ads are shown.
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard entry, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing us.
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything stored on your phone.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
